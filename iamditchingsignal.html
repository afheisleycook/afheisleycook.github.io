Public Notice

I am ditching Signal for Matrix.

I am formally moving my messaging away from Signal and embracing Matrix as my primary communication platform.

Signal was once a strong option, but I value open standards, federation, and user control more than closed ecosystem convenience. Matrix gives me the freedom to communicate in a more decentralized, future-proof, and interoperable way.

Why I am switching:

  • Matrix is decentralized and federated, not locked to a single corporate platform.
  • It supports open protocols and greater interoperability across ecosystems.
  • I want more control over my identity, privacy, and communication infrastructure.
  • Matrix aligns better with my values around autonomy, openness, and long-term resilience.

This is my declaration: I am leaving Signal and moving my conversations to Matrix. I encourage others to explore open, federated alternatives when they care about digital autonomy.
